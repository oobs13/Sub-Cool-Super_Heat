🔧 Identify the Pipes

Suction Line
Large pipe
Cold to touch
Low pressure
🔥
Liquid Line
Small pipe
Warm to touch
High pressure
💡 The larger insulated pipe is always the suction line. The smaller copper pipe is the liquid line.
⚠️ Safety First!
🛑 Always wear safety glasses and gloves when connecting gauges
🔌 Make sure the AC unit is RUNNING when taking readings
🌡️ Never touch hot pipes or compressor - they can burn you!
🔥 How to Measure Superheat
1 Connect BLUE hose to suction line (large pipe) service port
2 Read the LOW pressure gauge (PSIG)
3 Use your thermometer to measure the temperature of the suction line
4 Enter both values in the calculator
📍 Measure temperature 6 inches from the compressor on the suction line
💧 How to Measure Subcooling
1 Connect RED hose to liquid line (small pipe) service port
2 Read the HIGH pressure gauge (PSIG)
3 Use your thermometer to measure the temperature of the liquid line
4 Enter both values in the calculator (select Subcooling mode)
📍 Measure temperature near the outdoor unit on the liquid line
🇮🇶 Iraqi Climate Tips
☀️ In Iraqi summer (45-50°C), pressures will be HIGHER than normal
⏰ Best time to check: Early morning or evening when it's cooler
🌪️ Dust storms can clog filters - always check airflow first!
Practice makes perfect! Stay safe out there.
